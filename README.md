## When does the Ulta Black Friday sale start?

Online first, stores the Sunday before Thanksgiving. The leaked ad scan lays out the sequence: the ad drops on Wednesday, November 13; online deals go live Monday, November 18 at 8:00pm MST; stores open Sunday, November 24 at 10am local time. Weekly-ad trackers put the sale window at November 22 through 28, which matches Ulta's habit in recent years of kicking things off about a week before Thanksgiving.

Thanksgiving Day itself: closed. Black Friday: doors open at 6 a.m. If you want first crack at doorbusters without fighting a parking lot, the Monday-night online launch is the moment that matters. Exact dates shift a bit from year to year, so treat the November 13 ad leak as your signal to start watching.

## When Black Friday isn't actually the best time to buy at Ulta

Black Friday gets the headlines, but it's not the deepest sale on Ulta's calendar for every category. The Love Your Skin Event runs from the end of December through mid-January and slashes 50% off daily beauty steals — if you're after prestige skincare, that event frequently matches or beats the Black Friday doorbusters. The Big Summer Beauty Sale, starting at the end of June, takes around 30% off hair dryers, styling tools, dry shampoo and the Ulta Beauty Collection. Fall Haul arrives mid-September with up to 30% off select items across all categories.

The practical read: if the specific item you want appears on the leaked Black Friday ad, buy it during the sale week. If it doesn't, buying it at a smaller discount in November may cost you more than waiting six weeks for Love Your Skin.
